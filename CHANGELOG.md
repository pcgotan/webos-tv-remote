# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

First release.

- Finds LG webOS TVs on the local network and pairs with one "Allow" on the TV.
- Remote control: D-pad, OK, Back, Home, media keys, channel keys, TV settings.
- Mac keyboard as the remote (arrows, Return, Backspace, Esc) and typing into the TV's text boxes.
- Send to YouTube: play or queue a video in the TV's YouTube app, with a history of sent videos.
- All TV apps with icons, HDMI inputs, Live TV, volume and mute.
- On-demand screen capture, saved to the Desktop or copied.
- Message on the TV, sleep timer, power off and Wake-on-LAN power on.
- Global shortcuts and a menu-bar icon. Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel.
